Support

BucketShare for iPhone and iPad

Email shapingedge.apps@gmail.com. Please include your device, iOS version and the app version (Settings → About). Don't send screenshots with amounts or account details you'd rather keep private.

My data isn't syncing between devices

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices, and check that iCloud is allowed for BucketShare in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. Settings → Data in the app shows Sync on or Sync off.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Open Settings → Pro → Restore purchases, signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In iOS Settings, tap your name → Subscriptions → BucketShare. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

How do I move my data or keep a copy?

Settings → Data → Backup and import → Export backup saves a file you can keep anywhere and import later. Pro can also export spreadsheets (CSV).

Are the tax estimates exact?

No. They use published tables for your country and the pay you enter, as a guide. Your actual tax depends on your full situation.
